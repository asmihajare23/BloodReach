ABOUT BLOODCONNECT

Connecting People When It Matters.

BloodConnect is a CEP project created with the purpose of making blood donor connections simpler and more organized.

The platform provides a simple way for potential donors to register, patients or their families to submit blood requirements, and users to find blood-bank information.
