WHO WE ARE

About BloodConnect

A community-focused platform created to make blood donor connections simpler and more accessible.

OUR STORY

Connecting People Through Compassion.

BloodConnect is a Community Engagement Project designed to create awareness about blood donation and provide a simple digital platform for connecting potential donors with people who need blood.

Finding a suitable blood donor during an emergency can be difficult. BloodConnect aims to organize this process by bringing important information together in one place.

Through this platform, users can explore blood groups, search for potential donors, submit blood requests, and find information about blood centres.

HOW IT WORKS

Simple. Connected. Responsible.

1. Register: Potential donors can provide their basic information and blood group.

2. Search: Users can search for potential donors using blood group and location.

3. Request: People who need blood can submit a blood requirement through the platform.

4. Connect: Potential donors can respond to relevant blood requirements.
