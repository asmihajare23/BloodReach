BEFORE YOU REGISTER

Donate Responsibly.

Blood donation should always be carried out through an authorized blood centre or healthcare facility.

Eligibility to donate depends on factors such as age, health condition, medications and other requirements determined by qualified healthcare professionals.

Please consult an authorized blood centre to confirm your eligibility before donating.
